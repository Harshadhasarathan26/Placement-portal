Interview Etiquettes & Guidelines

Professional Dress Code

Wear formal or business casual attire. Ensure your clothes are well-ironed and you have a neat appearance. Subdued colors like white, light blue, black, or navy work best for initial impressions.

Strong Body Language

Maintain eye contact without staring. Sit up straight and avoid slouching. Use hand gestures moderately and greet the interviewer with a firm handshake (in-person) or confident vocal greeting (online).

General Instructions

Research the company heavily beforehand. Prepare examples that highlight your skills using the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result). Be honest when answering and arrive 10 minutes early.
